# Keyfocus

A keyboard-first focus workspace: a spatial canvas of **frames** that a person moves through almost entirely by keyboard, one keystroke at a time.

## Language

**Focus space**:
The whole workspace — the canvas, its frames, and the focus that moves between them. Every frame and note belongs to one focus space.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, app, workplace.

**Frame**:
A single workspace surface on the canvas, belonging to exactly one feature. One frame is active; every other one sits in the stack.
_Avoid_: Card, panel, window, tab.

**Feature**:
The category a frame belongs to — habits, reminders, focus music, notes, todo list, or pomodoro. A feature is the kind of work; a frame is one instance of it.
_Avoid_: Tool, app, module, widget.

**Active frame**:
The single frame shown on the main side — what the person is working in right now.
_Avoid_: Current frame, open frame.

**Stack**:
The ordered set of non-active frames, shown as a card stack on the left side. It is the person's recent context, newest first.
_Avoid_: List, history, backlog.

**Promote**:
To move a stack frame into the main side, making it active and pushing the previous active frame into the stack. Promotion reorders the stack; it is not a plain switch.
_Avoid_: Open, select, switch.

**Side**:
One of the three regions focus moves between: left (the stack), main (the active frame), right (the dock).
_Avoid_: Column, pane, region, panel.

**Command**:
An entry in the command palette. A command either creates a frame of a feature or enters search.
_Avoid_: Action, item, option.

**Command palette**:
The ⌘K overlay for creating frames and searching existing ones.
_Avoid_: Launcher, spotlight, omnibox.

**Feature panel**:
The flyout that lists the frames belonging to one feature, opened from the right dock.
_Avoid_: Drawer, sidebar, popover.
